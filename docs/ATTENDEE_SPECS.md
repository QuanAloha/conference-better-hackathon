# ATTENDEE_SPECS

## Pre-Conference
An attendee will see a link to sign up for a conference. When a attendee then clicks on the link they will enter in their details IE. Name, Phone, Email, and finally a question will be present asking attendees "What would you like to learn from this conference?" Attendees can then give a short response which will then be collected by the speakers. 

## At Conference
Attendees will be able to pull up the app during the talk. The app will display a live transcript of the talk, followed with a live translation if needed. The transcript is then saved on the user's device and will be availble for the attendees to search post-talk for keywords to then re-read the talk. When searching the transcript they keywords that are found will be highlighted for easy-read access. A feature the speakers will have access to is to present questions multiple choice, true or false, etc.., and the attendees will have a interactive answer sheet show up on their device and they will be able to submit their answers. answers can then be displayed live on a screen.

## Post-Conference
Attendees will be able to open their app, and have a collection of all the talks given at a conference they have attended. When they click on a talk it will have a saved transcription which will be translated if needed, they will then be able to have the same search availibity across the transcription. 

## Remote-Attendees
Scope creak... Users will be able to sync their talks to third-party note collection apps (Notion, Obsidian, etc...) They can also opt-in for RSS collections of other conferences with favored topics or ideas. The app will have a browse page which they can view talks given by speakers at conferences they were unable to attend. This will allow them to save them and access talks at any time. Remote-attendees can join in a live watch party with other remote-attendees and be able to watch the talk live followed by a live transcription and tranlsation. They will also have access to a live-chat section where they can chat with other attendees and ask questions. This will allow for a greater reach for speakers and organizers and also allow for continued engagment.

## Style Guide
Most users will be using their phone devices so the app needs to be clean and simple.
