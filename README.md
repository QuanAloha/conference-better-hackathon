# Ideas for the Hackathon

## An AI Agent for Transcribing the Workshops
Before the event, you would be able to set what you are looking to get out of the conference. This app could also be marketed towards the conference organizers. It could take all of the ideas that the event attendees are interested in and report back to the organizers how well those topics were taught on. 

## A Dock Scheduler for Building Up and Breaking Down
This is an app that the dock at the confrence could use to efficiently schedule the trucks that are coming into the dock to drop off and pickup the equipement. It would be driver focused allowing the driver to park their truck somewhere nice, and also help the driver come and listen into the confrernce that they are bringing all of the things for. 

## Near Group Hallway Sharing
Allows users to share their github and other parts of their projects, linked in, websites, and portfolios.
